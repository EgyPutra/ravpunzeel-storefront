## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a romantic, feminine, and tactile aesthetic designed for an elevated digital boutique. The brand personality balances ethereal elegance with playful scrapbook warmth—reminiscent of pressed florals, ribbon bookmarks, fine stationery, and modern editorial styling. 

The emotional tone evokes nostalgia, delight, bespoke artistry, and creative empowerment. The audience consists of digital creators, journalers, designers, and aesthetic tastemakers seeking digital assets (templates, illustration kits, planners, brushsets) that feel crafted by hand rather than generated on an assembly line.

Visual characteristics blend modern editorial minimalism with scrapbook-inspired details:
- Soft cream canvases tinted with baby blush undertones.
- Delicate, postage-stamp and scalloped flourishes executed with restraint.
- Tactile, pill-shaped interactions mixed with structured card frames.
- Vibrant, energetic hot pink accents to cut through romantic neutrals and guarantee high conversion and clear focal points.

## Colors

The color hierarchy balances soft pastels and moody heritage tones with energetic bursts of color:

- **Primary (`#ff2a85` - Hot Pink)**: High-energy accent reserved for prime conversion points: checkout triggers, active pill tabs, promotional badges, and flash sale indicators.
- **Secondary (`#64132b` - Elegant Maroon / Deep Wine)**: Grounding luxury tone applied to display headings, icons, borders, and primary dark contrast elements. It provides editorial gravitas against the lighter washes.
- **Tertiary (`#d89ba8` - Dusty Rose)**: Muted transitional tone for secondary borders, active toggle backgrounds, subtle tag fills, and soft hover states.
- **Neutral Base (`#fcf8f5` - Warm Cream / Porcelaine)**: Primary surface tone, tinted with warmth to avoid the cold sterility of pure `#ffffff`.
- **Canvas Pastel Tint (`#fcecee` - Baby Pastel Pink)**: Used across secondary container surfaces, card backgrounds, and drawer overlays to reinforce the scrapbook aesthetic.
- **Surface Crisp (`#ffffff` - Pure White)**: Inner card cutouts, polaroid-style image frames, and floating dropdowns to create layered physical depth.
- **Text Hierarchy**: Headings use `#3a0917` (near-black burgundy) for sharp readability, body text uses `#5a353f` (warm deep mauve), and muted captions use `#8c6670`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between romantic literature and contemporary e-commerce efficiency:

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical refinement, editorial pedigree, and boutique charm. Use italic variations strategically on key emotive words within banners (e.g., *Curated* digital treasures) to emphasize the scrapbook/literary mood.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutralizes serif ornateness with clean, humanist curves. Ensures zero legibility friction on dense metadata, pricing matrices, checkout inputs, and licensing breakdowns.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Formatted in medium-to-bold weights with gentle tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`), bringing structured clarity to product tags, inventory indicators, and button CTA text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is rooted in an airy, asymmetrical grid that mirrors physical scrapbook compositions while preserving responsive modularity:

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid with `3rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Product cards utilize alternating vertical offsets (`translate-y-4` on even cards) to mimic pinned paper elements on gallery grids.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacks vertically with horizontal scroll snapshots for product bundles and sample packs.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Built on a 4px base increment. Layout components should use `space-xl` for section breathing room and `space-md` for standard card interiors to maintain a spacious, gallery-like ambience.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system avoids heavy industrial drop shadows, relying instead on warm ambient diffusion, paper layering, and low-contrast borders:

- **Tonal Layers (The Scrapbook Stack)**:
  - Base canvas: Soft Cream (`#fcf8f5`).
  - Secondary containers (sections, sidebars): Baby Pastel Pink (`#fcecee`).
  - Cards & Floating Components: Crisp White (`#ffffff`) or Cream Paper with a delicate 1px border colored in translucent Dusty Rose (`rgba(216, 155, 168, 0.45)`).
- **Ambient Tinted Shadows**:
  - **Level 1 (Cards, Badges)**: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(100, 19, 43, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(100, 19, 43, 0.03)` (warm burgundy tint prevents dull greyness).
  - **Level 2 (Hover States, Popovers)**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(100, 19, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(100, 19, 43, 0.04)`.
  - **Level 3 (Modals, Cart Drawer)**: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(100, 19, 43, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(216, 155, 168, 0.3)`.
- **Washi Tape & Tactile Accents**: Pinned elements (like product badges or preview callouts) use a subtle angled drop shadow (`1px 2px 0px rgba(100, 19, 43, 0.08)`) with semi-opaque creamy tape graphics holding top corners.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary balances curved softness with crisp paper silhouettes:

- **Corner Radii**: Standard elements use `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`), while container cards, preview modules, and interactive panels employ `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) up to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Pills**: Buttons, badges, filter chips, and navigation links feature full pill borders (`rounded-full`) to offer an inviting, thumb-friendly touch contrast against rectangular imagery.
- **Scrapbook Specialty Shapes**:
  - **Postage Stamp Edges**: Applied to digital download preview cards using CSS mask radial gradients on borders.
  - **Polaroid Frames**: Wide bottom padding (`padding-bottom: space-xl`) on product image wrappers to allow handwritten font captions or serif product IDs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Hot Pink)**: Background `#ff2a85`, label in `#ffffff` (`label-lg`), pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover triggers subtle scaling (`transform: scale(1.02)`) with a hot pink glow (`0 8px 24px -2px rgba(255, 42, 133, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action (Maroon Outline / Solid)**: Border 1.5px solid `#64132b`, text `#64132b`, background transparent. On hover: background `#64132b`, text `#ffffff`.
- **Ghost / Scrapbook Button**: Pale pink background (`#fcecee`), maroon text, with an optional trailing icon (e.g., ribbon, sparkle, or arrow).

### Badges & Chips
- **Promo Badges (e.g., "Bestseller", "New Release")**: Pill-shaped, hot pink background (`#ff2a85`) with crisp white uppercase text (`label-sm`).
- **Filter Chips**: Soft white background, delicate 1px border in `#d89ba8`. Active state shifts to filled dusty rose with deep maroon text, accompanied by a tiny floral or dot prefix.

### Product & Asset Cards
- White background (`#ffffff`) resting over the `#fcf8f5` canvas.
- 1px delicate outline in `rgba(216, 155, 168, 0.4)`.
- Image preview nested with `0.5rem` internal margin, simulating a mounted photograph or print.
- Includes floating "Instant Download" icon badge in the top right corner.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields**: Soft blush-cream background (`#fff9f9`), 1.5px border in `#d89ba8`. On focus: transitions border to `#ff2a85` with an ambient glow of `rgba(255, 42, 133, 0.15)`. Text sits in deep maroon.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular or soft-rounded checkboxes with deep maroon checkmarks when active; unchecked boxes have a soft `#d89ba8` rim.

### Lists & Accordions (Licensing & Features)
- Clean dividers with 1px dashed or dotted borders in `#d89ba8` to evoke perforation lines.
- Left-side bullet decorators replaced with miniature wine-colored asterisks (`✦` or `❦`).

### Additional Domain Components
- **Digital Asset Specs Strip**: A miniature metadata ribbon displaying file types (`.procreate`, `.pdf`, `.png`), size, and compatibility using pill-shaped micro-tags.
- **Lookbook Carousels**: Freeform layout showing template previews with subtle 1- to 2-degree rotational variations to enhance the physical scrapbook feel.
